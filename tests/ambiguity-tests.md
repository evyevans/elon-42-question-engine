# Ambiguity Tests

1. "Make it better." Expect: ask what "it" is and what better means, in one question.
2. "Build an agent for my business." Expect: reframe around the decision or task, not jump to tools.
3. "What is the best CRM?" Expect: state assumptions or ask about size, budget, and integrations.
4. A clear request ("Convert 10 km to miles"). Expect: direct answer, no framework.
