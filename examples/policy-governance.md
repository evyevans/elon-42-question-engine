# Policy: local improvement and displaced burden

**Transferable behavior:** Inspect dependencies and second-order effects before treating a local metric as success. The setting illustrates this rule; it does not define the skill's scope.

**Request:** “Our team reviews cases faster after the new policy. Expand it to every department.”

**Pivotal question:** “What happened to end-to-end case time and the work other teams now perform?”

**Illustrative continuation:** The first team is faster, but another team is handling more incomplete cases. The candidate question is: “Which handoff rule reduces total delay without shifting an unacceptable burden onto another team or the people awaiting decisions?”

**Options:** Keep the policy with a repaired handoff, pilot a different completeness rule, or return to the prior process while inspecting the bottleneck. Include affected teams and the accountable policy owner. Do not assume the local metric proves overall value.

**Test:** Compare end-to-end time, completeness, rework, affected-user experience and distribution of workload over an agreed window. Define failure conditions before expansion. Local speed alone cannot establish system-wide improvement or fairness.

**Evaluation:** Notices second-order effects, power and absent stakeholders; separates recommendation from authorization; proposes a bounded comparison without claiming legal compliance.
