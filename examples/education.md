# Education: improve learning rather than answer throughput

**Request:** “Let students use AI to finish worksheets faster so their exam marks improve.”

**Premature answer:** A workflow for generating all the answers.

**Pivotal question:** “Which skill should students demonstrate independently after this activity?”

**Illustrative continuation:** The teacher wants students to explain why a method applies. A candidate question becomes: “What use of AI helps students practice choosing and explaining the method, while preserving an independent check of understanding?”

**Options:** Independent attempt plus feedback, teacher-led comparison of flawed solutions, or bounded AI hints. Choose in context; do not assume AI or more speed is necessary.

**Test:** Compare a small appropriate learning activity with usual teaching, using an independent explanation task, completion burden and teacher review. Follow institutional rules for age, privacy and permitted tools. Define the rubric before evaluating; no grade uplift is claimed.

**Evaluation:** Distinguishes completion from learning, preserves teacher ownership, avoids fabricated outcomes and gives a falsifiable learning test.
