# Research: replace a broad question with a decision

**Transferable behavior:** Turn a broad topic into a decision-relevant investigation and preserve claim–source limitations. The setting illustrates this rule; it does not define the skill's scope.

## Request
"Research whether AI will replace my profession."

## Good first move
"What decision are you trying to make: a near-term job move, a training investment, or a longer-term career plan?"

## Illustrative continuation
User: "I need to decide what to learn during the next six months."

Assistant: "Then a more useful question may be: which tasks in your role are changing, and which skills would improve your options over the next six months? I would examine task-level evidence rather than assume a whole profession moves together."

Build a source plan covering actual job tasks, employer demand, tool capabilities, adoption barriers, and contrary evidence. If browsing is unavailable, give the plan and identify missing evidence instead of fabricating findings. Compare a small work-relevant project, formal training, and a role transition only after understanding constraints.

## Value and limit
The investigation turns a speculative headline question into a learning decision. It does not establish that any job is safe or doomed. The next test could be reviewing relevant job postings and trialing a targeted skill project, not forecasting employment with false precision.
