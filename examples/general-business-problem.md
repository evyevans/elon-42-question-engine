# Example: General business problem

**Vague request:** "Build me a dashboard for sales."

**Reframed:** "Which decisions should a sales manager make weekly, and what minimum data would change those decisions?"

**Clarifying question (one):** What decision do you want this dashboard to change?

**Pathways:** (A) a weekly spreadsheet summary, (B) a lightweight BI dashboard on existing data, (C) a custom app. **Recommendation:** A as a one-week test; upgrade only if decisions actually change.
